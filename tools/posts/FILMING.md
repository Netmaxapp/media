# Emotional TikToks to film

Two ideas based on the examples you sent. Both need a real person on camera, so they have to be filmed. Use a phone, vertical, at 1080×1920. Send me the clips and I'll edit them: captions 3 words at a time, cuts between sentences, licensed music, and the caption with the credit.

The pull is the same in both: a friendship that's far away, and that feeling is still worth keeping. NET-MAX only shows up in the caption. Putting a product pitch inside an emotional video breaks the mood.

## 1. The walking talk (like the selfie-walk example)

**Setup:** you walk outside, holding the phone at arm's length. It's one take. Use natural light, and record the sound on the phone.

**Script** (say it to the camera, as if you're talking to the friend):

> we live seven hours apart, so there's no point in us even talking anymore
> *(beat)*
> is something i would never say.
> you're asleep when i'm free, i'm in class when you're up,
> and i'd still take a ten minute call at eight in the morning
> over anyone who lives close.

- **The turn:** leave a real half-second pause before "is something i would never say". That pause is the turn the video depends on.
- **Length:** about 15 s. Don't speed it up.
- **The time zone:** seven hours is Indianapolis to Athens in November. If your friend lives somewhere else, tell me where and I'll swap in the right number of hours.
- **Words on screen:** the script itself, 3 words at a time, in sentence case.
- **Caption idea:** "for the friends in other time zones. NET-MAX shows everyone you know with their local time, so the 8 AM call is easy to find. launches on iOS in january, waitlist link is in our Instagram bio"

## 2. Message in a bottle, filmed for real (like the bottle example)

The stock version (`out/tiktok-7-hours-ahead.mp4`) tells this story with ocean footage. Filming it yourself makes it stronger. These are the five shots, each 2–3 s:

1. **Night, at your desk:** you write a note on paper: "thinking about you. call this week?" Show it in close-up.
2. **The bottle:** you roll the note up and push it into a clear glass bottle, then cork it.
3. **Low angle, at a puddle or creek:** you set the bottle down in the water and let go.
4. **The bottle drifting:** a few seconds of it floating in the water.
5. **Morning light:** a second person's hand picks the bottle up. Use a friend's hand, in a different place. They pull the note out.

- **Caption, on screen the whole time:** "How it feels to text someone 7 hours ahead of you"
- **Timestamps:** I'll add "11:42 PM in Indianapolis" over shot 1 and "Delivered 6:42 AM in Athens" over shot 5, both calculated for the posting week.
- **Afterwards:** pick the bottle back up after filming. Don't leave glass in the water.

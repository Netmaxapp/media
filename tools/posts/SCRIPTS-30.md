# 30 video scripts, about 15 seconds each

Ten scripts for each of the three themes:

- **A. Behind the scenes:** who we are and how NET-MAX gets built.
- **B. What NET-MAX helps with:** the problems it solves, each with a tip people can use today.
- **C. People in the age of AI:** community, relationships and your network.

All 30 follow the new-content-rules skill:
- The hook is on the first frame and the payoff lands before the 10-second mark.
- One ask, the waitlist, on the end card only.
- No invented numbers or stories.
- Every time is calculated with `tools/carousels/time.mjs`.

Each script runs about 15 s at a natural pace, with roughly 30 to 38 spoken words.

**Fill these in before filming.** Anything in [brackets] is a blank for you:
- your first names
- your sport
- your own examples, where noted

The repo doesn't have them, and the rules don't let us make them up.

**Times.** The time-zone examples use New York City as home and November clocks. They hold until US clocks change on March 14, 2027. After that, rerun `node .claude/skills/new-content-rules/scripts/dst.mjs <post date>` and recheck the numbers.

**Every script ends on the same 3-second card:**
> NET-MAX, free on iOS in January
> Waitlist link in our Instagram bio

**Formats:**
- **Filmed:** a phone video of you, either talking to camera or as B-roll.
- **Motion:** made with our renderer (`tools/videos` or `tools/posts`).
- **Stock:** free-licence Mixkit clips only, with the same actor across all the beats of one video.

On-screen captions for speech show 3 words at a time.

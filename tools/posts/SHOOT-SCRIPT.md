# NET-MAX shooting script

This is everything we film ourselves, in one document:

- **Part 1: reshoots.** The videos you rejected, rewritten so you can film them with your own phone and people.
- **Part 2: shot lists** for every filmed script in `SCRIPTS-30.md`.

The motion-graphic scripts (A10, B1, B4, B5, B10, C2, C8) aren't here, because I render those without filming.

## How to shoot (applies to every video)

- **Camera:** phone held vertical, 4K or 1080p, 30 fps. Back camera when someone else films, front camera for selfies.
- **Light:** face a window or a lamp. Don't shoot with a window behind you.
- **Sound:** record on the phone, in a quiet room. Say each line clearly, then wait 2 seconds before the next one.
- **Takes:** at least 3 takes of every spoken shot, plus 5 seconds of silent B-roll for each shot.
- **Framing:** keep faces and anything important in the middle of the frame. TikTok covers the right edge and the bottom quarter.
- **Leave out:** captions, music, filters and stickers. I add captions 3 words at a time, plus licensed music, the end card and the times.
- **Upload:** name each clip `<script>_<shot>_<take>.mov`, e.g. `R2_shot1_take2.mov`, and upload them in the chat. Tell me which take you like best, if you have one.
- **Lines:** say the lines as written, or in your own words with the same meaning. Anything in [brackets] is yours to fill in.

# Part 2: Shot lists for the 30 scripts

The lines and timings are in `SCRIPTS-30.md`. These lists say what to film.

## A. Behind the scenes

**A1. Who we are.** At the gym or field, then at your desk.
1. Both of you to camera, side by side: "We're [name] and [name]. We play [sport]." (Medium shot, outside the gym.)
2. B-roll of practice at 6:30 AM: the alarm going off, walking in the dark, warm-up.
3. B-roll at night: the laptop open, both of you at the desk.
4. One of you to camera at the desk: "At night we build NET-MAX: everyone you know on one map, with their local time."

**A2. Why we're building it.** One of you, to camera, one take, somewhere quiet.
1. "We kept losing people."
2. "Friends from trips, teams, semesters abroad. All of them in different time zones." Then your own example: [one friend you lost touch with, and where they live].
3. "So we're building the app we wanted for ourselves."

**A3. A week of building NET-MAX.** B-roll only, no talking, 2 seconds per shot.
1. The alarm, 6:30 AM.
2. Practice.
3. A notebook in class, with sketches in the margin.
4. The laptop at night.
5. The two of you at the table on Sunday.

**A4. We use AI, and we say so.** At the desk.
1. A screen recording of Claude Code in the terminal while you work. Keep anything private off screen.
2. To camera: "It drafts our posts too, this one included."
3. You reading the screen and scrolling: "We read every line before anything ships."

**A5. Which green.** Skit at the desk, colour picker open on the laptop.
1. Over the shoulder: [Name 1] points: "This green."
2. [Name 2] leans in and points at the same swatch: "No. This green."
3. A pause, then [Name 1], deadpan: "Those are the same."
4. Both to camera: "We went with this one." Make sure the colour picker shows 39FF88.

**A6. The Sunday meeting.** At the table where you meet.
1. To camera: "One meeting a week, every Sunday."
2. "Same three questions every time: [question 1], [question 2], [question 3]." Hold up a notebook page with the three questions written on it.
3. "Then it's practice Monday at 6:30."

**A7. No fake screens.** To camera at the desk.
1. "You won't see a fake NET-MAX screen from us."
2. "The first screen we post will be the real build, running on a real phone."
3. "Until then, you get the people, the problem and the build."

**A8. Ideas start in the margins.**
1. Close-ups of the class notebook, slowly turning pages of globe sketches.
2. A hand sketching a new one.
3. Cut to the laptop at night, with code on screen.

**A9. Our office is a frat house desk.** Handheld, one take.
1. Walk from the front door to the desk, then sit down: "Our office is a desk in a frat house."
2. "Practice in the morning, class all day."
3. "NET-MAX after dinner, most nights until [time]."

## C. People in the age of AI

**C1. AI helps us build.** At the laptop.
1. To camera, with code on screen behind: "We use AI every day to build NET-MAX."
2. Close the laptop, pick up the phone: "Deciding who to call this week stays with us."
3. Scroll contacts: "Pick yours before Sunday."

**C3. Networking is the follow-up you send that night.**
1. Walking out of an event or a career fair at dusk, with a lanyard if you have one.
2. Typing: "great meeting you today. sending that article tomorrow."
3. The next morning, sending the article link.

**C4. Your flight's cancelled in Chicago.** A real airport if you're flying soon. Otherwise use free-licence stock footage of a departures board.
1. The departures board.
2. To camera: "If you can name someone there, that's your network."
3. Writing a list: city, name, how you met.

**C5. Bring back one group chat.**
1. Scroll an old group chat. Blur names and faces.
2. Send an old photo, then type: "found this. still doing the reunion in march?"

**C6. "We should visit sometime".** To camera.

**C7. Our bet on the AI era.** To camera, somewhere with people in the background, like a campus or a café.

2. "So ask one person this week:"

**C9. Three people to text before the year ends.** To camera, counting on your fingers. Each line is a hard cut.
1. "Three people to text before the year ends."
2. "Someone who helped you this year."

4. "Someone you met once and liked."

**C10. Send a voice note instead.**
1. To camera: "Can't find a time to call? Send a voice note."
2. Holding the record button and talking into the phone.
3. The phone going into your pocket, then you walking off.
